About AlphaSignalsAi

A practical project focused on clarity, structure and learning.

Our approach

AlphaSignalsAi brings together chart-reading concepts and trade-planning utilities in a compact, phone-friendly interface. The goal is to help users understand their process rather than promise profits.

Core principles

  • Confirm signals instead of chasing every candle
  • Keep stop-loss and target levels visible
  • Use multiple timeframes for context
  • Be transparent about demo versus live data
